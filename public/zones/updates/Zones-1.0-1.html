Zones 1.0

The first release of Zones, a menu bar app for snapping windows into clean layouts.

  • Hold Shift while dragging a window to snap it into a zone.
  • Choose a preset or custom layout for each display.
  • Build your own layouts in the layout editor.
  • Use zone controls with VoiceOver and the keyboard.
  • Get update checks in the background, with a prompt before anything installs.
