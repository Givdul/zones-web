# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Zones Web is for broad Mac users first: people who want cleaner window management with almost no setup friction and want to understand the product within seconds. Power users should still find the site credible, but they are secondary.

## Product Purpose

The site presents focused Givdul Mac utilities and moves visitors toward the right app page or App Store/download action. Success is a visitor quickly understanding what each utility does and trusting it enough to continue.

## Brand Personality

Calm, sharp, premium. The voice is confident and minimal, with short lines that explain the product visually before relying on dense copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup landing pages, Apple-clone marketing patterns, chatty promotional language, dense explanatory sections, decorative motion, and template card grids.

## Design Principles

- Let interaction and animation explain the product before copy does.
- Prefer one strong section over several explanatory ones.
- Use slogans and short supporting lines instead of dense marketing paragraphs.
- Keep layouts distinctive and premium without imitating Apple page patterns directly.
- Use motion to clarify interaction and honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep contrast strong, semantics clean, and focus behavior deliberate. Honor reduced motion preferences for all nonessential animation.
